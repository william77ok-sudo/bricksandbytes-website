Private beta · Your experience matters

Help shape Homeowner Build Pathway.

We are looking for homeowners planning or undertaking an extension, loft conversion, garage conversion or substantial refurbishment in England.

Try the pathway independently, at your own pace, and help us make it clearer and more useful.

  • An initial 15–20-minute independent trial.
  • Optional further use during a 14-day private testing window.
  • Honest feedback about usefulness, confusing guidance and anything that does not work.

Interested in taking part?

Email us to express interest. If you wish, include your name, type of home project, current project stage, device you would use and the email address you would like considered for access.

All these details are optional. Please do not send home addresses, plans or sensitive documents at this stage.

Email us about beta testing

No configured mail app? Write to support@bytesandbricks.co.uk with the subject “HBPD beta testing enquiry”.

Spaces are limited. Emailing expresses interest; access is provided only after individual approval.

We use the information you choose to email to respond to your enquiry and consider you for private testing. See our privacy information.
