The longer-term ambition

From one plot
to bigger possibilities.

Sheds are the starting point. Beyond them is a roadmap for a much larger world.

  • Houses and mansions
  • Commercial buildings and skyscrapers
  • Futuristic landmarks
  • A shared online neighbourhood

These are future ideas, not confirmed release commitments. A live multiplayer world is not currently being offered.

Play and real projects

Same curiosity.
Different purpose.

The game explores construction through play. Homeowner Build Pathway helps people understand the stages of a real home extension.

Game budgets, timings and construction scenarios are simplified and fictional. Gameplay is not construction training or professional advice for real building work.
