Who it is for

Your home.
Your questions.
A clearer next step.

For homeowners planning an extension in England who want to understand the process before and during building work.

You do not need a construction background. The guide helps you prepare for conversations with the people working on your home.

Guidance to support your project.

HBPD does not replace designers, engineers, builders or Building Control. Requirements depend on your property and work; the app does not guarantee costs, permission, compliance or outcomes.

The tailored pathway currently covers England. It is not a guide for commercial building projects.
