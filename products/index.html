What is coming, and when.

Homeowner Build Pathway and Extension Feasibility are our first launch priorities. These are provisional planning windows from our October review, not confirmed release dates or offers to purchase. They depend on testing, feedback and launch checks and will be reviewed as work progresses.

Before products go on sale

Each launch needs validated customer workflows, clear pricing, a working purchase and access journey, appropriate privacy and terms, and a support process. We will start with a small paid pilot, learn from customers and widen availability when the product is ready.

Sentinel is longer-term research. Spend Review / Ledger Reconciler and contract tools remain concepts. PropertyPilot / DREA require further assessment. None has a confirmed public release date.
