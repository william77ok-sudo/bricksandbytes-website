Who we want to learn with

  • Families coordinating everyday care across several people
  • Independent caregivers managing regular handovers
  • Small care teams willing to test a carefully scoped workflow

What happens next

A screened, supported process

  1. Try the fictional demo.
  2. Send a short, non-sensitive application.
  3. Join a fit and safeguarding conversation.
  4. Receive a written pilot scope before real use begins.

Important

Do not send personal or medical information in your application.

The public demo uses fictional information only. Applying does not grant access to a live care service. No participant should enter real personal, health or confidential information until Bytes & Bricks has confirmed that the production pilot, permissions and data safeguards are ready.

Pilot application

Tell us about the coordination problem—not the person receiving care.

We use this information only to assess whether Relay may be a suitable pilot. Do not include names of care recipients, diagnoses, medication, medical history or other sensitive details.
